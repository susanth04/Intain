Intain Campus FinTech Challenge 2026 · Prototype Phase

Loan Performance
Intelligence Engine

End-to-end ML pipeline for mortgage delinquency, default, and prepayment prediction — with SHAP explainability, anomaly detection, and stress scenario simulation.

↗ GitHub Repository ↓ Download submission.csv

Problem

Mortgage servicers must make critical decisions — forbearance approvals, loss mitigation, modification offers — without timely, forward-looking signals. Conventional reporting tells you what happened; it doesn't tell you what will happen to a loan in the next 12 months.

This system ingests monthly loan performance records, detects data quality anomalies, and predicts the 12-month probability of default and prepayment at the individual loan level — so servicers can act before losses crystallize.

Pipeline Overview

Seven sequential tasks, fully automated via python run_all.py:

1

Data Intelligence & Profiling

81,189 panel rows profiled for missingness (MCAR + MNAR patterns), outliers, rule violations, and train/test distribution drift (PSI, KS). Batch DQ score: 0.846 / 1.000.

2

Feature Engineering & Time-Aware Split

35 features including rolling balance stats, DPD lags, and categorical encodings. Strict temporal split: Train ≤ month 20, Val ≤ month 25, Test = months 26–30. Leakage check runs automatically.

3

Predictive Models

LightGBM (champion) vs. Logistic Regression (baseline) for four binary targets. Early stopping on the validation set; calibration via Sigmoid CalibratedClassifierCV.

4

Survival & Hazard Modelling

Kaplan-Meier curves by credit band, Cox PH (C-index 0.5617), and competing-risks CIF plot for Default vs. Prepayment.

5

Anomaly & Exception Detection

Isolation Forest + rule-violation scoring. 6.1% of the test-window records flagged as exceptions. Automated recommended_action assigned per loan-month.

6

Explainability Layer

SHAP TreeExplainer for all binary classifiers. Global importance, local TP/FP/FN examples, and per-prediction entropy scores.

7

Scenario & Stress Simulation

Three scenarios (base, adverse credit, high prepayment) with segment-level default projections by credit band, state, and servicer.

Known Limitations

These limitations are deliberately disclosed — they represent honest engineering. Source: reports/model_card.md.

Val→Test AUC Drop (~13 points on default)

Validation ROC-AUC for default is 0.9277 vs. test 0.7968 — a ~13pp drop. The synthetic data has an adverse macro-shock in months 15–18 (entirely within the training window). The model may overestimate risk in the calmer test period (months 26–30), producing more false positives at inference time.

Macro-Shock Stationarity

Scenario stress-testing shows limited sensitivity across scenarios because the macro-shock was absorbed into training distribution. A true stress test would require out-of-distribution shock injection.

Multiclass SHAP Not Available

The SHAP explainability layer for the multiclass next_state predictor fails at runtime due to a multidimensional output array (35, 7). SHAP explanations are restricted to the four binary classifiers.

Earlier Root-Cause Detour (AI Dev Log)

An initial attempt used Beta-distribution rank rescaling to force variance into probability outputs. This was wrong — it fabricated signal without fixing the upstream target derivation bug. The bug (simulation truncation at month 30 causing zero positives in val/test) was identified and fixed properly. See ai_dev_log/AI_DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md for the full account.
